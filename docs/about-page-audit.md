# ABOUT Page Reference Audit

Audit date: 2026-06-21

## Reference Pages

- `https://en.jdbio.com/pages/ceo-message`
- `https://en.jdbio.com/pages/overview`
- `https://en.jdbio.com/pages/global-network`

Chrome DevTools MCP was used to inspect page structure, computed typography, spacing, images, and company sub-navigation.

## Reusable Layout Findings

- Full-width corporate banner with a strong blue company identity.
- Horizontal sub-navigation immediately beneath the banner.
- CEO message presented as a large two-column portrait-and-letter composition.
- Introduction page organized around company purpose, business areas, mission, values, and vision.
- Global Network page separates regions and markets into clearly labeled groups.
- White content canvas, dark navy headings, restrained dividers, and wide section spacing.

The reference HTML, proprietary components, photographs, and company copy were not reproduced.

## MEDIVISTA Adaptation

The MEDIVISTA ABOUT page now contains:

1. Corporate hero.
2. CEO Message.
3. Company Introduction.
4. Four business-focus areas.
5. Vision and two mission statements.
6. TECHNOLOGY, TRUST, QUALITY, and BEAUTY values.
7. Global Network by region.

The previous Contact Us tab and final contact CTA were removed as requested. Header/footer access to the site's global Contact page remains part of the shared site navigation, but Contact is not presented as an ABOUT subsection.

## Content Development

Existing themes of trust, clear product communication, and global connection were expanded into a fuller company narrative. The copy remains:

- English-first;
- B2B oriented;
- inquiry and partnership focused;
- free of unsupported clinical, regulatory, market-leadership, and guaranteed-efficacy statements.

The CEO name uses the existing company information already published by MEDIVISTA: `WANG ZIYUE`.
